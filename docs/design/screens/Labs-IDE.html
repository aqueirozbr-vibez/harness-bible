Worked numbers

The scenario agent raises a work order for pump P-101 in 11 steps. Three write to external systems. At 2,000 queries a day and a worker loss of 0.4% per step ill illustrative, a task meets a failure with probability 4.3% ƒ L38.

That is 86 interrupted tasks a day ƒ L41. A loop that restarts from step 1 makes 117 duplicate side effects a day ƒ L47.

A durable workflow with an idempotency key on every write activity repeats none. Lab 21 reproduces this by killing the worker at step 7.

A retried write without a key is a second write. Section 26.3 gives the sensor that catches it.
